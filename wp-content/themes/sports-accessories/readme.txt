== Description ==

The Sports Accessories is a powerful, modern, and responsive theme tailored specifically for sports retailers, fitness brands, athletic gear sellers, and eCommerce businesses focused on sports accessories. Designed to highlight a wide range of products—such as sports gear, gym accessories, athletic wear, and training equipment—this theme is perfect for showcasing everything from fitness trackers, resistance bands, yoga mats, and sports water bottles to compression sleeves, gym gloves, sports bags, and sports safety gear. It suits both brick-and-mortar stores expanding online and new digital-only fitness brands. With clean layouts, high-quality product display options, and seamless WooCommerce integration, it ensures a user-friendly shopping experience for customers seeking outdoor training gear, workout accessories, or team sports essentials. Built with SEO optimization, fast load speeds, and mobile responsiveness in mind, the theme helps brands improve visibility and reach a wider audience. It includes visually striking elements like a full-width banner, featured product sections, client testimonials, blog integration, and engaging call-to-action (CTA) buttons. Whether you sell boxing accessories, sports timers, ski gear, sports towels, or hydration packs, this theme makes your site visually appealing and conversion-ready. It also supports product filters, customizable homepages, and integration with social media, enabling easy promotion of fitness gear, performance wear, and sportswear accessories. 

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload Theme and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this theme support any plugins? =

Yes, the theme is integrated to work with following plugins:
* One Click Demo Import: This plugin is recommended to use for demo importing purpose.
* GiveWP – Donation Plugin and Fundraising Platform: This plugin is recommended to use for adding donation forms and accepting donations.
* Contact Form 7: This plugin is recommended to use for contact forms on contact page.
